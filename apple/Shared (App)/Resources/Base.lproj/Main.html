Welcome to Guard!

Guard checks the images on the pages you browse and flags the ones that look AI-generated, violent, or explicit — right where you see them, without uploading anything.

Permissions

Guard is a Safari extension, so Safari has to let it read the pages you visit. That access stays on your device — it's what lets Guard check images as they load.

  • Open Settings → Safari → Extensions
  • Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions
  • Turn on Guard, then set its website access to Allow
  • Open Safari → Preferences → Extensions
  • Open Safari → Settings → Extensions
  • Turn on Guard, then set its website access to Allow

This opens Settings on Guard’s own page — Safari’s extension list is a few taps away, as above.

Settings

The last step happens in Safari: choose what Guard should look for, what it does when it finds something, and whether to link your Elhio account.

Guard opens its setup in a Safari tab. You can come back here any time — this step waits until you’ve finished it.
